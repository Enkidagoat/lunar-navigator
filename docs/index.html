Lunar Navigator

Geometric Sun-center and Earth-center elevation comparison for published south-polar candidate points.

Geometric center of the Sun and Earth above a smooth spherical tangent plane. Not terrain-aware, not apparent-disk visibility, not power, thermal, or communications assessment. Coordinates provisionally interpreted as planetocentric in MOON_ME_DE421.

Data and method

  • Three published points from Peña-Asensio et al. (provisional MOON_ME_DE421 interpretation).
  • Hourly UTC samples, inclusive calendar days, no interpolation.
  • Skyfield + pinned DE421 kernels.
  • Simultaneous visibility = both geometric centers above the smooth tangent plane at the same sample.
